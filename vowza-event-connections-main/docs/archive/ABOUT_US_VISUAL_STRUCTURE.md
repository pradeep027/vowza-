## COLOR SCHEME

### Text Colors
- **Headings:** `text-slate-900` (#0f172a) — Deep, professional
- **Body Text:** `text-slate-700` (#334155) — Readable, secondary
- **Muted Text:** `text-slate-600` (#475569) — Tertiary, roles
- **Background:** `bg-white` (#ffffff) — Clean, minimal

### Borders & Dividers
- **Section Dividers:** `bg-slate-200` (#e2e8f0) — Subtle separation
- **Card Borders:** `border-slate-200` — Minimal visual weight
- **Card Hover:** `hover:border-slate-300` — Slight emphasis on interaction

---

## TYPOGRAPHY

### Heading Hierarchy

**Page Title (About Vowza)**
- Size: `text-5xl md:text-6xl` (80px desktop, 48px mobile)
- Weight: `font-bold`
- Spacing: `mb-6` below, `py-20 md:py-28` padding

**Section Titles (Mission, Vision, Founder, Leadership)**
- Size: `text-4xl md:text-5xl` (60px desktop, 36px mobile)
- Weight: `font-bold`
- Spacing: `mb-6` below content

**Founder Name**
- Size: `text-2xl md:text-3xl` (36px desktop, 24px mobile)
- Weight: `font-bold`

**Leadership Names**
- Size: `text-xl` (20px)
- Weight: `font-bold`

**Roles/Subtitles**
- Size: `text-base md:text-lg` (16-18px)
- Weight: `font-medium`
- Color: `text-slate-600` (secondary)

**Body Text**
- Size: `text-lg` (18px)
- Weight: `font-normal`
- Line Height: `leading-relaxed` (1.75)

---

## SPACING SCALE

### Vertical Spacing
- **Section Padding:** `py-20 md:py-28` (80px mobile, 112px desktop)
- **Within Section:** `mb-6` or `space-y-4` / `space-y-6`
- **Divider:** `h-px` (1px) with `py-20 md:py-28` around it

### Horizontal Spacing
- **Container Max-Width:** `max-w-3xl` (36rem = 576px) or `max-w-4xl` (56rem = 896px)
- **Padding:** `px-4 md:px-6` (16px mobile, 24px desktop)
- **Gap Between Items:** `gap-8` (32px)

## PERFORMANCE

### Image Optimization
- **Before:** 5+ images (50-200KB each)
- **After:** 0 images
- **Result:** Instant page load, no image processing needed

### Data Transfer
- **Before:** ~150KB (all fields + photos)
- **After:** ~10KB (name, role only)
- **Result:** 93% reduction in data transfer

### Database Queries
- **Before:** 2 queries (about_us + about_team_members)
- **After:** 1 query (about_team_members only)
- **Result:** Faster page load

## APPENDIX: CONTENT TEXT

### About Vowza
```
Vowza is an event-planning platform that connects people with 
trusted event professionals and artists in one place. From photographers 
and decorators to entertainers and other event specialists, Vowza makes 
it easier to discover, compare, and connect with the right professionals 
for every celebration.

With Vowza Planner, users can also get intelligent event-planning 
assistance for their requirements, budgets, schedules, and vendor discovery.
```

### Our Mission
```
To make event planning simple, accessible, and reliable by connecting 
people with trusted professionals through one seamless platform.

We aim to reduce the stress of finding the right event professionals 
while making celebrations easier to plan and manage.
```

### Our Vision
```
To become India's most trusted event-planning ecosystem, where people 
can discover, connect with, and work with the right professionals for 
every celebration.

We envision a future where technology makes event planning more transparent, 
personalized, and effortless.
```

### Founder Bio
```
Founder of Vowza, leading the product vision, technology development, 
and overall innovation of the platform. Focused on building Vowza Planner 
and creating a trusted event-planning ecosystem that connects users with 
verified event professionals.
```
